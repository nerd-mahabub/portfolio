What kind of support can you provide?

I can help with general virtual assistance, business tasks, social media support, video editing, content creation, and graphic design. We can shape the scope around what would be most useful to you.

Can you support ongoing social media work?

Yes, this can include content planning, preparing assets, scheduling, and keeping a steady publishing workflow. We’ll agree on platforms, responsibilities, and a rhythm that works for your team.
